AkaTerminal is an Ubuntu 26.04 terminal for Android. Open the app and you are in an Ubuntu shell: no Termux, no separate X11 app, no proot-distro commands.

<b>Features:</b>
• Ubuntu without root (PRoot), or as a chroot on rooted phones
• Zsh with Oh My Zsh, tabs, two rows of extra keys, a Paste key, tap-to-open links
• Hold wakelock and Exit from the notification, for long jobs with the screen off
• Your phone's folders in ~/storage, and Ubuntu's home in Android's Files app
• Optional XFCE desktop over an embedded X11 server with sound, added during setup or later

<b>Note:</b> The Ubuntu rootfs downloads from GitHub after an in-app opt-in. No data is collected.

AkaTerminal is a fork of FluxLinux.
