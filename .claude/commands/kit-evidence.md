argument-hint: "ticket or change reference"
---
Record the commit, dirty/untracked working-tree status, acceptance criteria, exact commands and
exit outcomes, relevant screenshot paths, APK identity/variant when applicable and remaining
unverified behavior. Reuse existing test outputs from this revision rather than rerunning every
review. Store a concise record with the Sidequest ticket when available; during standalone review,
keep it in .claude/kit/evidence for later attachment. Do not mark an unchecked criterion as passed.
Keep raw logs private and inspect for credentials before sharing or committing artifacts.
